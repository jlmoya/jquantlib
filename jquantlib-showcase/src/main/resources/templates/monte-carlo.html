Monte Carlo Convergence

JQuantLib prices the option by simulating thousands of price paths. As the path count grows, the estimate converges to the analytic Black–Scholes value and the statistical error shrinks like 1/√N. A Sobol low-discrepancy sequence converges markedly faster than a pseudo-random one.
