JQuantLib, in action

Interactive demonstrations of JQuantLib — a pure-Java port of QuantLib v1.42.1 — spanning option pricing, Greeks, Monte Carlo, fixed income, yield curves and market calendars.

Every number on this site is computed live by the library. Built with Spring Boot.

📈
Options & Greeks

Price European calls/puts with the analytic Black–Scholes engine; live delta, gamma, vega, theta and rho; a 7-engine cross-check; and payoff/Greek charts.
🇺🇸
American Options

Early-exercise premium across Barone-Adesi/Whaley, Bjerksund/Stensland, Ju, binomial trees and finite differences.
🎲
Monte Carlo

Watch the MC price converge to Black–Scholes as paths grow, with 95% confidence bands — and see Sobol low-discrepancy beat pseudo-random.
🔎
Implied Volatility

Back the volatility out of a market price with the library's solver, and see the price-vs-vol curve it inverts.
💵
Fixed-Rate Bonds

Clean/dirty price, accrued interest, yield and cash flows on a discounting curve — plus the classic price-vs-yield curve.
📉
Yield Curve

Build an interpolated zero curve from pillar rates and read off discount factors, zero rates and forward rates across 30 years.
📅
Calendars & Schedules

Holidays, business-day counts and conventions across TARGET, US, UK and Japan, plus coupon-schedule generation.
🧪
Sample Programs

Run the bundled jquantlib-samples programs — swaps, FRAs, repos, convertibles, discrete hedging, Bermudan swaptions — live in your browser.

Tip: each demo posts your inputs to a JSON endpoint backed by JQuantLib (e.g. /api/option/european) and renders the response. Open your browser's network tab to see the library's raw output.
